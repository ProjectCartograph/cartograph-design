# DD-0005 The entry is the change

**Status:** Proposed
**Date:** 2026-10-17
**Requirements:** [P1](../../reference/requirements/people-and-first-use.md#p1-start-from-the-change-with-no-choice-first), [P2](../../reference/requirements/people-and-first-use.md#p2-one-thing-per-screen), [E1](../../reference/requirements/change-and-evaluation.md#e1-how-the-change-is-expected-to-happen), [R2](../../reference/requirements/shape-of-the-record.md#r2-kind-is-a-label-given-after)

## Context

In-person tests found people gave up at the first screen after onboarding, and finished only the guided walk. Across domains, the order of questions is the same: what is wrong, what should be different and for whom, how it will come about, how we will know, then who decides and what it costs ([round 3, g4 section 3](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md)). g4 weighs three centres a person could start from.

| Entry | Makes easy | Makes hard |
|---|---|---|
| The decision and its case | Approval, audit, boards | Steering over time; it reads as form filling |
| The change wanted (theory of change) | The why and how-will-we-know; matches people's own words | Money and authority need their own place |
| The unit of work | Money, dates, exchange | Output-shaped; brings back classification first |

## Decision (proposed)

The first walk asks for the **change**: what is wrong, what should be different, for whom, how it is expected to come about, and how we will know. The piece of work, its label and its case are built from those answers; the decision and its case are what the record later produces for approval ([DD-0010](DD-0010-views-are-renderings.md)).

This decision is **conditional on a test with people** ([the validation milestone](../../reference/milestones.md)). The tests so far support it but recorded no data.

## Consequences

- Home offers "say what you want to change" and "pick up where you left off".
- The kind is suggested after the first answers, never asked first.
- If the test shows people start more easily from the work, only this entry changes; [DD-0004](DD-0004-one-record-for-work-at-any-scale.md) stands either way.
