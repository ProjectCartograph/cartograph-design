# DD-0005 The entry is the change

**Status:** Proposed
**Date:** 2026-10-17
**Requirements:** [P1](../../reference/requirements/people-and-first-use.md#p1-start-from-the-change-with-no-choice-first), [P2](../../reference/requirements/people-and-first-use.md#p2-one-decision-per-screen), [E1](../../reference/requirements/change-and-evaluation.md#e1-how-the-change-is-expected-to-happen), [R2](../../reference/requirements/shape-of-the-record.md#r2-the-kind-is-a-label-given-after)

## Context

People in the field gave up at the first screen and finished only a guided walk ([FIELD](../../reference/bibliography.md)). Across domains, the questions come in one order: what is wrong, what should be different and for whom, how it will come about, how we will know, then who decides and what it costs ([HMT-CASES](../../reference/bibliography.md), [KELLOGG](../../reference/bibliography.md), [NPC](../../reference/bibliography.md), [GDS-MANUAL](../../reference/bibliography.md)). A first walk could be organised around any of three things.

| Entry | Makes easy | Makes hard |
|---|---|---|
| The decision and its case | Approval, audit, boards | Steering over time; it reads as form filling |
| The change wanted | The why and the how-will-we-know, in people's own words | Money and authority need their own place |
| The unit of work | Money, dates, exchange | Output-shaped; brings back classifying first |

## Decision (proposed)

The first walk asks for the **change**: what is wrong, what should be different, for whom, how it is expected to come about, and how we will know. The piece of work, its label and its case are built from the answers; the decision and the documents that ask for it follow from the record ([DD-0010](DD-0010-views-are-renderings.md)).

This decision is **conditional on a test with people** ([milestone M5](../../reference/design-process.md)).

## Consequences

- Home offers to start from a change, and to pick up where the person left off.
- The label is suggested after the first answers, never asked first.
- If people start more easily from the work, only the entry changes; [DD-0004](DD-0004-one-record-for-work-at-any-scale.md) stands either way.
