# Stages and cycles

Where a piece of work is in its life, and how its life is shaped.

Each requirement is a statement that can be checked. Its status starts Proposed and becomes Approved, Changed or Dropped in review ([how to review a requirement](../../how-to/review-a-requirement.md)). Sources are in [the bibliography](../bibliography.md).

### S1 Where it is in its life

A person can see where each piece of work is in its life, using the organisation's own names for the points.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Every domain has a life cycle; their names and number differ.
- **Accepted when:**
  - Every piece of work shows its current point, in the workspace's words.
- **Holds across:** UK public sector, public services, construction, development finance
- **Evidence:** [GOVS002](../bibliography.md) 6.3; [GDS-MANUAL](../bibliography.md); [RIBA](../bibliography.md); [WB-CYCLE](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** a session with people, technical and not, recorded with the people's trace

### S2 Stages are data

Stages are data: their names, their number, the decision at each, who decides and what must be known by then are set by a profile, and having no stages is valid.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Life cycles range from none to eight stages, and the standards allow any.
- **Accepted when:**
  - A profile defines the stages and the decision at each.
  - A workspace with no stages works.
- **Holds across:** project standards, construction, software, UK public sector
- **Evidence:** [EC-PM2](../bibliography.md); [RIBA](../bibliography.md); [SCRUM](../bibliography.md); [IPA-BENEFITS](../bibliography.md) 7.2; [MSP-MOP](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model's schemas and their examples

### S3 Cycles without gates

Work steered by cycles (a sprint, a quarter, a season, a financial year) keeps decisions, risks and reviews on that cycle without passing gates.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Products, services and objectives are steered by regular review, not by gates.
- **Accepted when:**
  - A piece of work can be given a cycle, and its reviews fall due on it.
- **Holds across:** software, corporate strategy, public sector elsewhere
- **Evidence:** [SCRUM](../bibliography.md); [OKR](../bibliography.md); [OMB-A11](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model's schemas and their examples

### S4 A group's own decision points

A group of work (a programme, a portfolio) has its own decision points and reviews through its life, apart from its members'.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** A programme's case is reviewed before each tranche and each year; a portfolio reviews its members on its own cycle.
- **Accepted when:**
  - A programme or portfolio has decisions and reviews of its own.
- **Holds across:** UK public sector
- **Evidence:** [HMT-CASES](../bibliography.md) 5.13 to 5.14; [NAO-PROGRAMMES](../bibliography.md); [TEAL](../bibliography.md) chapter 12
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model's schemas and their examples
