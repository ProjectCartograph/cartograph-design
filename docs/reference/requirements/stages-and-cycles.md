# Stages and cycles

Where a piece of work is in its life, and how its life is shaped.

Each requirement below is a statement that can be checked. Its status moves from Proposed to Approved, Changed or Dropped as it is reviewed ([how to review a requirement](../../how-to/review-a-requirement.md)).

### S1 Where it is in its life

A person can see which point in its life each piece of work is at, and the list of points is the organisation's own.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Every domain has a life cycle; their names differ.
- **Domains:** every domain read
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) L1; [g6](../../../research/round-3/g6-domains.md) section 4
- **Verified by:** a test with people (the walk-first test script)

### S2 Stages are data

Stages are data: names, number, the decision at each, who decides and what must be known by then are set by a profile, and 'no stages' is valid.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** PM2 has four phases, RIBA eight stages, DMAIC five, Scrum none.
- **Domains:** project and programme standards, construction, software and services, international development, public sector
- **Sources:** [g6](../../../research/round-3/g6-domains.md) DR-5, DR-35; [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) L7, L8
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### S3 Cycles without gates

Work steered by cycles (a sprint, a quarter, a season, a fiscal year) keeps decisions, risks and reviews on that cycle without passing gates.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Products, services and OKRs are steered by cadence, not gates.
- **Domains:** software and services, corporate strategy, public sector
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) L8; [g6](../../../research/round-3/g6-domains.md) section 4
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### S4 A group's own decision points

A group of work (a programme, a portfolio) has its own decision points and reviews through its life, apart from its members'.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Programme tranches and portfolio reviews are the group's, not its members'.
- **Domains:** public sector, project and programme standards
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) L9; [r2p](../../../research/round-2/programmes.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records
