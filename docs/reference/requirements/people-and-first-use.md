# People and first use

How a person meets Cartograph: the first screen, the walk, the words and the help. These carry the in-person finding that people gave up when faced with everything at once, and finished only the guided walk.

Each requirement below is a statement that can be checked. Its status moves from Proposed to Approved, Changed or Dropped as it is reviewed ([how to review a requirement](../../how-to/review-a-requirement.md)).

### P1 Start from the change, with no choice first

A first-time person starts from the first screen with no choice of kind, level or method beforehand, and says what they want to change before anything else.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** People gave up when asked to classify work in the product's nouns; every domain asks for the problem and the change first.
- **Domains:** design research, every domain read
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C1, C5; [g5](../../../research/round-3/g5-current-state.md) C19
- **Verified by:** a test with people (the walk-first test script)

### P2 One thing per screen

Each screen of a walk asks one thing and offers one clear next step; the other controls in view are few enough that the step stands out.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The only screen people finished had 7 controls and 1 primary action; the median screen had 49 and 2.
- **Domains:** design research
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C2; [r1u](../../../research/round-1/tools-and-ux.md)
- **Verified by:** `just audit` on the screens, and a test with people

### P3 Leave and come back

A person can leave a walk at any point, return to where they stopped, and see what is left.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Definition happens over days and between meetings; the GOV.UK task list pattern exists for this.
- **Domains:** design research
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C3; [r1u](../../../research/round-1/tools-and-ux.md)
- **Verified by:** a test with people (the walk-first test script)

### P4 The person's words

Every term shown is a word the person would use; the standard term is one step away.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Domains name the same thing differently, and jargon was part of what frightened people.
- **Domains:** design research, every domain read
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C4; [g5](../../../research/round-3/g5-current-state.md) C17
- **Verified by:** a test with people (the walk-first test script)

### P5 The full workspace one step away

The full workspace is one step from any walk, and coming back returns to the same place.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Power users must lose nothing; a walk must not become a second product.
- **Domains:** design research
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C6; [g5](../../../research/round-3/g5-current-state.md) C18
- **Verified by:** a test with people (the walk-first test script)

### P6 Home names the next thing

Home names the single most useful next thing, from the state of the record.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** An open question with no direction left people stuck on Home.
- **Domains:** design research
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C9
- **Verified by:** a test with people (the walk-first test script)

### P7 Finishable unaided by anyone

A person with a screen reader, a keyboard only, or little confidence with computers can finish the first walk unaided.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Public bodies are bound to accessibility standards, and the tool serves non-technical people.
- **Domains:** design research, public sector
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C8
- **Verified by:** a test with people (the walk-first test script)

### P8 Help by need, from where it is needed

Each page has one Diataxis job (learn, do, look up, understand) and links to the others; the facts for a field are reachable from the field.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Mixed pages serve no need well; people stuck on a field need its facts there, not elsewhere.
- **Domains:** design research
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C10, C11, C12; [r1](../../../research/round-1/charter-standards.md) Diataxis guide
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### P9 Save anything; completeness only when authorising

A person can save at any point; only a step that authorises or hands off asks for completeness, and it says what is missing.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Staged cases approve outlines before full cases exist; refusing a save loses work.
- **Domains:** public sector, project and programme standards
- **Sources:** [g5](../../../research/round-3/g5-current-state.md) C6; engine ADR 0035
- **Verified by:** an automated check in the build

### P10 Depth in proportion

The depth asked of a piece of work follows what the person and their approver agree it needs; nothing appears that was not asked for or needed.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The Green Book, the Magenta Book, NPC and OKR practice all ask for proportion; a hobbyist and a national government use one tool.
- **Domains:** public sector, corporate strategy, software and services, charity and philanthropy
- **Sources:** [g1](../../../research/round-3/g1-business-case.md) G1-04; [g5](../../../research/round-3/g5-current-state.md) C7; [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) P1, P2, P5
- **Verified by:** a test with people (the walk-first test script)
