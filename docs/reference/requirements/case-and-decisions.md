# The case and the decisions

The justification for work, how it grows, and the decisions taken on it.

Each requirement below is a statement that can be checked. Its status moves from Proposed to Approved, Changed or Dropped as it is reviewed ([how to review a requirement](../../how-to/review-a-requirement.md)).

### D1 A case that grows

The justification (need, options, benefits, cost, risk, how it will be delivered) is one record, revised before each decision, held at several levels of completeness.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The Green Book, New Zealand, the World Bank and RIBA all grow one case through stages.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development, project and programme standards, construction
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) J1, J2; [g1](../../../research/round-3/g1-business-case.md) section 2
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### D2 Five questions at any scale

The same five questions can be asked at any scale: is there a need, does it give value, is it viable, is it affordable, can it be done.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The five case model is scale-free; only the depth changes.
- **Domains:** public sector, project and programme standards
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) J3; [g1](../../../research/round-3/g1-business-case.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### D3 If nothing is done

The record holds what happens if nothing new is done, in a sentence, or says plainly that it is not known.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The counterfactual is the reference every appraisal and evaluation measures against.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development, impact measurement
- **Sources:** [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) T5; [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C16
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### D4 Options considered

Options considered, including doing nothing, and the reason one was chosen, can be recorded before commitment; they are asked for when public or a funder's money is sought.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Required for public money (Green Book, NZ); optional for a hobbyist. Must where the profile is for public or funded investment.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) J4; [g5](../../../research/round-3/g5-current-state.md) C9
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### D5 A decision is a record

A decision is a record: who decided, in what role, when, the outcome (go, go with conditions, revise, defer, stop), the reasons, and which version of the case it rested on.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Every life cycle read turns on recorded decisions; today there are none.
- **Domains:** every domain read
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) L2, L3; [g1](../../../research/round-3/g1-business-case.md) section 6
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### D6 Conditions on a decision

Each condition on a decision has an owner, a due point and what it holds back, and the record shows when it is met.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Approvals with conditions are the norm at gates and boards.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) L4
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### D7 Stopping is ordinary

Stopping, pausing and cancelling are ordinary outcomes with a reason; the work stays readable.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** GovS 002 and the Green Book treat stopping as a legitimate decision.
- **Domains:** public sector, project and programme standards, international development
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) L5, C2
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### D8 A baseline, and changes to it

The approved baseline (scope, cost, dates, benefits) is kept; the current plan is compared with it; a change to the baseline is its own recorded act with its reason and authoriser, apart from correcting a typing error.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Change control and the NAO's reset question need the baseline and its changes apart.
- **Domains:** public sector, project and programme standards, international development
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) J6, J7
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### D9 The chain upward

A piece of work inherits from the level above (the strategy, the programme's case) and can show the chain upward.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Cases nest: a project's strategic case is largely its programme's.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development
- **Sources:** [g1](../../../research/round-3/g1-business-case.md) section 6
- **Verified by:** review of a rendering against its source

### D10 Portfolio decisions

A portfolio's decision to fund, hold or stop each member is a decision record, with criteria and the objective it serves when the portfolio uses them.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The Teal Book, SAFe and ISO 21504 make portfolio decisions the portfolio's output.
- **Domains:** public sector, software and services, project and programme standards
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) L9; [r2f](../../../research/round-2/portfolios.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records
