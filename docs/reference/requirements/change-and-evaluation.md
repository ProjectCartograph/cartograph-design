# How change happens, evidence and evaluation

The theory of change, assumptions, evaluation questions and learning.

Each requirement below is a statement that can be checked. Its status moves from Proposed to Approved, Changed or Dropped as it is reviewed ([how to review a requirement](../../how-to/review-a-requirement.md)).

### E1 How the change is expected to happen

A person can say how the change is expected to happen, as steps a reader can follow, with what must hold at each step, for any piece of work, without a programme existing.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The Magenta Book, Kellogg, NPC and Horizon Europe all start here; today only a programme has a pathway.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development, charity and philanthropy, research funding, impact measurement
- **Sources:** [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) T1, T2, T8; [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C17
- **Verified by:** a test with people (the walk-first test script)

### E2 Assumptions that can be tested

Each assumption can be marked for how much it matters and how well it is evidenced, has an owner and a date or event to look again, and the critical, weakly evidenced ones can be read first.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Long work rests on assumptions that age; testing the riskiest first is GOV.UK's and the Magenta Book's advice.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development, charity and philanthropy, software and services
- **Sources:** [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) T3; [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) J5; [r2p](../../../research/round-2/programmes.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### E3 Who is affected, and would it have happened anyway

The record says who experiences the change, for more than one group, and whether the change would have happened anyway.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The Impact Management Project and the Green Book's distributional analysis ask both; a hobbyist does not need them.
- **Domains:** impact measurement, international development, public sector
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C19, C20; [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) B7
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### E4 Decisions the evidence must inform

Before delivery, a person can list the decisions the evidence must inform, who takes each and when, and the questions the organisation wants answered, each tied to a decision or a weak point.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Evaluation that informs no decision is waste (Magenta Book, US learning agendas).
- **Domains:** public sector, international development, charity and philanthropy
- **Sources:** [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) Q1, Q2
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### E5 Evaluation in proportion

How much evaluation is proportionate, and why, is recorded; evaluation design is never needed to finish a first definition.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Proportion is the Magenta Book's first principle; asking too early repeats the overload problem.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development, charity and philanthropy
- **Sources:** [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) P1, P2
- **Verified by:** a test with people (the walk-first test script)

### E6 A dated plan for checking

The plan for checking is dated before outcome data is analysed; later changes keep the reason.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Pre-registration guards against reading the data to fit the claim.
- **Domains:** public sector
- **Sources:** [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) G2
- **Verified by:** an automated check in the build

### E7 Findings change decisions, and lessons are found

When findings arrive, the decision taken because of them is recorded and linked to the evidence; lessons can be found by later work in the same area.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Every source closes the loop; inspection without adaptation is pointless (Scrum).
- **Domains:** public sector, international development, charity and philanthropy, software and services
- **Sources:** [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) Q6, Q7, L1
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### E8 Evaluation lenses as views

Evaluation lenses (the OECD six, the Magenta Book's types) are optional views over the record, not required fields.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The lenses differ by domain; built in, they would tie the core to one.
- **Domains:** international development, public sector
- **Sources:** [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) L4, section 8
- **Verified by:** review of a rendering against its source

### E9 The theory keeps its history

The theory of change keeps its history: what it said earlier and why it changed.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The value of a theory of change is in revising it (Kellogg, Magenta Book).
- **Domains:** public sector, international development, charity and philanthropy
- **Sources:** [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) T7
- **Verified by:** an automated check in the build
