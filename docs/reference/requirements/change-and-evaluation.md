# How change happens, evidence and evaluation

The theory of change, assumptions, the questions evaluation asks, and how findings are used.

Each requirement is a statement that can be checked. Its status starts Proposed and becomes Approved, Changed or Dropped in review ([how to review a requirement](../../how-to/review-a-requirement.md)). Sources are in [the bibliography](../bibliography.md).

### E1 How the change is expected to happen

A person can set out how the change is expected to happen, as steps a reader can follow with what must hold at each, for any piece of work.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Evaluation guidance, logic models and funders' pathways to impact start here, and evaluators rebuild the theory where it is missing.
- **Accepted when:**
  - Any piece of work can hold a theory of change.
  - Each step can name what must hold for it.
- **Holds across:** UK public sector, evaluation, charity, research funding
- **Evidence:** [HMT-MAGENTA](../bibliography.md) 2.2; [OECD-CRITERIA](../bibliography.md) relevance; [OECD-APPLYING](../bibliography.md) chapter 4; [KELLOGG](../bibliography.md); [NPC](../bibliography.md); [EC-HORIZON](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** a session with people, technical and not, recorded with the people's trace

### E2 Assumptions that can be tested

Each assumption can be marked for how much depends on it and how well it is evidenced, has an owner and a point to look at it again, and the most critical, least evidenced ones can be read first.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Work rests on assumptions that age; testing the riskiest first is the advice of digital service and evaluation guidance, and reviews look back at how accurate assumptions were.
- **Accepted when:**
  - An assumption has an owner, how much depends on it, its evidence and when to look again.
  - Assumptions can be listed with the most critical and least evidenced first.
- **Holds across:** UK public sector, public services, international development
- **Evidence:** [HMT-MAGENTA](../bibliography.md); [GDS-MANUAL](../bibliography.md) alpha; [IPA-BENEFITS](../bibliography.md) 7.76; [EC-PCM](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model's schemas and their examples

### E3 Who is affected, and would it have happened anyway

The record can say who experiences a change, for more than one group, and whether the change would have happened anyway.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Impact and evaluation frameworks ask both; a hobbyist does not need them.
- **Accepted when:**
  - A result can name the groups it affects.
  - A result can state how far the change is the work's own.
- **Holds across:** impact investment, evaluation, UK public sector
- **Evidence:** [IMPACT-FRONTIERS](../bibliography.md); [OECD-CRITERIA](../bibliography.md) effectiveness; [HMT-GREEN](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model's schemas and their examples

### E4 Decisions the evidence must inform

Before delivery, a person can list the decisions the evidence must inform, who takes each and when, and the questions to be answered, each tied to a decision or a weak point in the theory.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Evaluation that informs no decision is wasted effort.
- **Accepted when:**
  - An evaluation plan lists questions, each tied to a decision or an assumption.
- **Holds across:** UK public sector, public sector elsewhere
- **Evidence:** [HMT-MAGENTA](../bibliography.md) 2.2, 6.2; [OMB-A11](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model's schemas and their examples

### E5 Evaluation in proportion, and possible

How much evaluation is proportionate, and why, is recorded, with an early check that the work can be evaluated given its data and access; evaluation design is never needed to finish a first definition.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Proportion is the first principle of evaluation guidance, and an early evaluability check avoids plans that cannot be carried out.
- **Accepted when:**
  - An evaluation plan records its proportion and the reason.
  - A first definition can be saved with no evaluation plan.
- **Holds across:** UK public sector, evaluation
- **Evidence:** [HMT-MAGENTA](../bibliography.md) 1.9; [OECD-CRITERIA](../bibliography.md) principle two; [OECD-APPLYING](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** a session with people, technical and not, recorded with the people's trace

### E6 A dated plan for checking

The plan for checking is fixed with a date before outcome data are analysed, and later changes keep their reason.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Fixing the plan first guards against reading the data to fit the claim.
- **Accepted when:**
  - An evaluation plan has the date it was fixed; later versions record why they changed.
- **Holds across:** UK public sector
- **Evidence:** [HMT-MAGENTA](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** an automated test in the build

### E7 Findings change decisions, and lessons are found

When findings arrive, the decision taken because of them is recorded and linked to the evidence, and lessons can be found by later work in the same area.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Every source closes the loop from evidence to decision, and benefits reviews look for lessons for the next piece of work.
- **Accepted when:**
  - A finding can link to the decision it informed.
  - Lessons can be searched by topic and by the goals they touch.
- **Holds across:** UK public sector, software
- **Evidence:** [HMT-MAGENTA](../bibliography.md) 6.2; [NISTA-GATES](../bibliography.md) Gate 5; [IPA-BENEFITS](../bibliography.md) 7.76; [SCRUM](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model's schemas and their examples

### E8 Evaluation lenses as views

Evaluation lenses (the OECD criteria, the Magenta Book's types of evaluation) are views over the record, optional by default and required where a profile says so.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The OECD warns against applying its criteria mechanically, while letting institutions require some.
- **Accepted when:**
  - A lens shows what the record holds for each of its questions.
  - A profile can make a lens required.
- **Holds across:** evaluation, UK public sector
- **Evidence:** [OECD-CRITERIA](../bibliography.md); [OECD-APPLYING](../bibliography.md) chapter 3; [HMT-MAGENTA](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** comparing a rendered view with the record it came from

### E9 The theory keeps its history

The theory of change keeps its history: what it said earlier, and why it changed.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The value of a theory of change lies in revising it.
- **Accepted when:**
  - Each revision of a theory of change is kept with its reason.
- **Holds across:** charity, UK public sector
- **Evidence:** [KELLOGG](../bibliography.md); [HMT-MAGENTA](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** an automated test in the build
