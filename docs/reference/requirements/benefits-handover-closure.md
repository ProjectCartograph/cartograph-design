# Benefits, handover and closure

What happens after delivery: benefits, acceptance, closure and the look back.

Each requirement below is a statement that can be checked. Its status moves from Proposed to Approved, Changed or Dropped as it is reviewed ([how to review a requirement](../../how-to/review-a-requirement.md)).

### B1 Benefits that are owned and dated

Each benefit has an owner, a measure, a baseline, a target and the date or event it is expected by; benefits that cannot be priced are held as fully as those that can.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** GovS 002 and the benefits guidance require each; most benefits in public work are not priced.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development, project and programme standards
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) B1, B2
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### B2 Disbenefits

Disbenefits are recorded beside benefits, with who bears them.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** GovS 002 and PRINCE2 count harm; omitting it biases the case.
- **Domains:** public sector, project and programme standards
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) B3
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### B3 From benefit to work and back

A reader can follow the chain from a benefit to the objective it serves and the work that should bring it, and back.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The benefits map is how a board sees what its money buys.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) B4
- **Verified by:** review of a rendering against its source

### B4 Readings after closure

Readings continue after the work closes until a review records the benefit realised, off track or stopped.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Benefits fall late; Gate 5 and completion reports look after delivery.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development, impact measurement
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) B5
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### B5 Accepted at handover

At handover a named owner accepts the result, the benefit tracking, and each open risk, issue and action; the acceptance is recorded.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Gate 4, ITIL transition and RIBA's handover all record acceptance.
- **Domains:** public sector, software and services, construction
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) H1, B6, R7
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### B6 A closing record and a look back

Closing records what was delivered against what was approved, what was not and how it will be handled, who answers for what remains, what was learned, and the review planned after.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** GovS 002's closure, PM2's end report and the World Bank's completion report.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development, project and programme standards
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) C1, C4; [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) L2
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records
