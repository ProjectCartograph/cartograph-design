# The shape of the record

What a record of work is: one shape at any scale, with its kind as a label, its links, and one place for each concept.

Each requirement below is a statement that can be checked. Its status moves from Proposed to Approved, Changed or Dropped as it is reviewed ([how to review a requirement](../../how-to/review-a-requirement.md)).

### R1 One shape at any scale

One record shape serves one person's single idea and a national strategy; a person never moves to a larger form to add a field.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Every domain has the same core; IATI's activity is one record at any scale.
- **Domains:** international development, every domain read
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C30; [g6](../../../research/round-3/g6-domains.md) section 3
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### R2 Kind is a label, given after

The kind of work (project, programme, portfolio, service, grant, product) is a label given after the person has said what it is, suggested by the tool, and can be changed without losing anything.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Classifying first is where people stopped; the scale is the publisher's choice in the open standards.
- **Domains:** international development, design research
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C33; [g5](../../../research/round-3/g5-current-state.md) section 6 option A
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### R3 Work without an end

Work with no end (a service, a product, an ongoing programme) has the same core as work with an end, and its own life (planned, running, retired) with a reason for retiring.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Canada, ITIL and Scrum manage ongoing units, not only time-bound projects.
- **Domains:** public sector, software and services, international development
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C32; [g6](../../../research/round-3/g6-domains.md) DR-25; [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) H2
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### R4 Typed links between work

Parent and child links between pieces of work are typed, declared on one side and readable from both; a piece of work may serve more than one parent.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Shared components are real; one declared side keeps the record consistent.
- **Domains:** international development, project and programme standards
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C31; engine TAXONOMY D46
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### R5 Views, not copies

The same record can be read as several views (a charter, a business case, a logframe, a theory of change, a funder's form, an open-data file) that stay consistent because they are renderings, not copies.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Each audience accepts its own document; copies drift.
- **Domains:** international development, public sector, charity and philanthropy
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C34, section 4
- **Verified by:** review of a rendering against its source

### R6 One place for each concept

Each concept has one place: one place for how progress will be known, one list of parties with roles, one word for each thing.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Today 'how will we know' is asked in seven places and 'who' in eight.
- **Domains:** international development, project and programme standards
- **Sources:** [g5](../../../research/round-3/g5-current-state.md) section 3.2, C2
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### R7 Outputs, outcomes and benefits kept apart

The record keeps apart what is produced, what changes for people and the lasting benefit, and a reader can tell which an item is.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Confusing outputs with benefits is the error PM2, Kellogg and the OECD warn of most.
- **Domains:** international development, project and programme standards, charity and philanthropy, impact measurement
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C18; [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) D5
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### R8 One accountable role

Every result, decision, indicator and risk names one accountable role; roles are held in the record, and people are linked to roles outside it.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Every domain names one answerable person; roles survive staff change and protect privacy.
- **Domains:** every domain read
- **Sources:** [g5](../../../research/round-3/g5-current-state.md) C5; [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) L6
- **Verified by:** an automated check in the build
