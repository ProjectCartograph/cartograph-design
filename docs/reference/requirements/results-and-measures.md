# Results and measures

How progress is known: indicators, targets and readings.

Each requirement below is a statement that can be checked. Its status moves from Proposed to Approved, Changed or Dropped as it is reviewed ([how to review a requirement](../../how-to/review-a-requirement.md)).

### M1 A complete indicator

Each indicator holds what it measures, its unit, which way is better, a baseline or a stated unknown with the date it will be known, a target, its source, who collects it, how often and who checks it.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** USAID's indicator sheet, IATI, the logframe and the Magenta Book agree on these fields.
- **Domains:** every domain read
- **Sources:** [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) D1, D2; [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C22, C26; [r1p](../../../research/round-1/planning-and-data.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### M2 Readings apart from definitions

Readings are stored per period apart from the definition, with the value, whether it may still change, and an optional note and breakdown; a target never overwrites an actual.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** IATI keeps period targets and actuals apart; overwriting loses the record.
- **Domains:** international development, public sector, corporate strategy
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C23, C24; [g5](../../../research/round-3/g5-current-state.md) C3
- **Verified by:** an automated check in the build

### M3 Interim targets and what achievement means

An indicator can carry interim or per-period targets, and say what over- and under-achievement mean.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Results frameworks score against interim targets; OKRs grade against stretch.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development, corporate strategy
- **Sources:** [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) D3, D4
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### M4 Any kind of target

A target may be a value by a date, a threshold, a graded stretch or qualitative; an indicator may be quantitative or qualitative.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Canada uses thresholds, OKRs grade, Australia allows qualitative measures.
- **Domains:** public sector, corporate strategy, international development
- **Sources:** [g6](../../../research/round-3/g6-domains.md) section 4
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### M5 Adding up and breaking down

Indicators say whether they can be added across pieces of work, and can be broken down by group.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** IATI's aggregation status and WHO's disaggregation make roll-ups honest.
- **Domains:** international development, health, impact measurement
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C22; [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) D6
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### M6 Recognised indicator lists

An indicator from a recognised list (SDG, IRIS+, a WHO registry, a funder's set) names the list and code; an organisation's own says so. A programme or fund can declare common measures and see who reports them.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Comparison across organisations needs shared codes.
- **Domains:** international development, health, impact measurement, charity and philanthropy
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) C25; [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) G5; [g6](../../../research/round-3/g6-domains.md) DR-16
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### M7 Outcome measured only by outputs

A warning appears when an outcome is measured only by outputs.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The commonest results-framework error.
- **Domains:** international development, project and programme standards, charity and philanthropy
- **Sources:** [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) D5
- **Verified by:** an automated check in the build
