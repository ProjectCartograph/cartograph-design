# Risk and assurance

Risks, issues, dependencies, escalation, and independent review.

Each requirement is a statement that can be checked. Its status starts Proposed and becomes Approved, Changed or Dropped in review ([how to review a requirement](../../how-to/review-a-requirement.md)). Sources are in [the bibliography](../bibliography.md).

### A1 Owned risks on one scale

Every risk and issue has an owner, a response and a status that runs to closed, may threaten a piece of work, a result or a benefit, and uses a common scale so it can be compared and rolled up.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Risk guidance consolidates risks on one scale, and benefits assurance asks for risks to the benefits themselves.
- **Accepted when:**
  - A risk has an owner, a response, a status and a rating on the workspace's scale.
  - A risk can be linked to a benefit.
- **Holds across:** UK public sector
- **Evidence:** [HMT-ORANGE](../bibliography.md); [GOVS002](../bibliography.md) 7.6; [NISTA-BENEFITS](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model's schemas and their examples

### A2 Escalation to authority

A risk beyond its owner's authority is escalated to a named role that can decide, and the record shows when, and what was decided.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Escalation beyond tolerance is the standard control.
- **Accepted when:**
  - Escalating a risk names the role it goes to and records the response.
- **Holds across:** UK public sector, project standards
- **Evidence:** [GOVS002](../bibliography.md) 7.1; [HMT-ORANGE](../bibliography.md); [PRINCE2](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model's schemas and their examples

### A3 Appetite and tolerance

The governing body can state its appetite for each kind of risk, and tolerances for a piece of work; a breach shows.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Risk guidance asks for a stated appetite; many small organisations never state one.
- **Accepted when:**
  - A workspace can hold risk appetite by kind.
  - A piece of work can hold tolerances, and a breach is flagged.
- **Holds across:** UK public sector
- **Evidence:** [HMT-ORANGE](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model's schemas and their examples

### A4 Dependencies with owners

Dependencies between pieces of work are recorded with an owner and the effect if the other side slips.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Large work fails at its interfaces.
- **Accepted when:**
  - A dependency has an owner and a stated effect.
- **Holds across:** UK public sector, large projects
- **Evidence:** [GOVS002](../bibliography.md) 7.2; [FLYVBJERG](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model's schemas and their examples

### A5 Reviews are records, apart from approvals

A review is a record of when, what it looked at, who, what it found, and each recommendation with an owner and a status. The reviewer is not the author, and a review is never an approval.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Assurance reviews advise and approvers decide; independence between who produces and who assures is the rule.
- **Accepted when:**
  - A review cannot be recorded by the author of what it reviews.
  - A review has no outcome that approves.
- **Holds across:** UK public sector
- **Evidence:** [NISTA-GATES](../bibliography.md); [HMT-AQUA](../bibliography.md); [GOVS002](../bibliography.md)
- **Verified by:** an automated test in the build

### A6 Planned assurance

The assurance expected before each decision is planned, in proportion to risk, and shown beside the decision.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The UK standard asks for an integrated plan of assurance and approvals.
- **Accepted when:**
  - A decision point can list the reviews expected before it.
- **Holds across:** UK public sector
- **Evidence:** [GOVS002](../bibliography.md) 4.2
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model's schemas and their examples
