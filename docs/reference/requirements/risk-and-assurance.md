# Risk and assurance

Risks, issues, dependencies, escalation and independent review.

Each requirement below is a statement that can be checked. Its status moves from Proposed to Approved, Changed or Dropped as it is reviewed ([how to review a requirement](../../how-to/review-a-requirement.md)).

### A1 Owned risks on one scale

Every risk and issue has an owner, a response and a status to closed; risks use a common scale so they can roll up from parts to programme to portfolio.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The Orange Book's consolidation needs one scale.
- **Domains:** public sector, project and programme standards, construction
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) R1, R4
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### A2 Escalation to authority

A risk beyond its owner is escalated to a named role with authority, and the record shows when and what was decided.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Tolerance-based escalation is GovS 002's and PRINCE2's control.
- **Domains:** public sector, project and programme standards
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) R2
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### A3 Appetite and tolerance

The governing body can state its appetite for each kind of risk and tolerances for a piece of work; breaches show.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** The Orange Book asks for appetite; small organisations rarely state one.
- **Domains:** public sector, project and programme standards
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) R3; [r2l](../../../research/round-2/large-projects.md)
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### A4 Dependencies with owners

Dependencies between pieces of work are recorded with an owner and the effect of the other side slipping.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Large work fails at its interfaces.
- **Domains:** project and programme standards, construction
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) R6
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### A5 Reviews are records, apart from approvals

A review is a record: when, what it looked at, who, what it found, and each recommendation with an owner and status. The reviewer is not the author; a review is never called an approval.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Gate reviews advise; approvers decide; independence is the Aqua Book's rule.
- **Domains:** public sector, project and programme standards, charity and philanthropy
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) A1, A2, A5
- **Verified by:** an automated check in the build

### A6 Planned assurance

The assurance expected before each decision is planned and read next to the decision, in proportion to risk.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** GovS 002's integrated assurance and approvals plan.
- **Domains:** public sector
- **Sources:** [g3](../../../research/round-3/g3-life-cycle.md) A3, A4
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records
