# Domains and configuration

What differs between domains, held as settings rather than built in.

Each requirement below is a statement that can be checked. Its status moves from Proposed to Approved, Changed or Dropped as it is reviewed ([how to review a requirement](../../how-to/review-a-requirement.md)).

### X1 The organisation's own words

An organisation sets its own words for levels, kinds, stages and roles; the standard terms stay one setting away.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Terminology is the first difference every domain shows.
- **Domains:** every domain read
- **Sources:** [g5](../../../research/round-3/g5-current-state.md) C17; [g6](../../../research/round-3/g6-domains.md) section 4
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### X2 The shape of the results hierarchy

The depth and shape of the results hierarchy (two levels, a tree, a logframe's four rows, perspectives) is a setting.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** OKRs have two levels, the logframe four rows, Canada a fixed tree, the balanced scorecard perspectives.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development, corporate strategy
- **Sources:** [g6](../../../research/round-3/g6-domains.md) DR-2, DR-4
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### X3 The approval route

The approval route is a setting, and 'no formal route' is valid.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Boards, ministers, steering committees and teams' own cadence all approve.
- **Domains:** every domain read
- **Sources:** [g6](../../../research/round-3/g6-domains.md) section 4
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### X4 Profiles as starting points

Profiles bundle words, levels, stages, routes, required parts and calendars as a starting point (for example public investment, project delivery, product team, charity, research, improvement), and can be changed.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** An idea from the domain survey, not yet tested with people.
- **Domains:** every domain read
- **Sources:** [g6](../../../research/round-3/g6-domains.md) section 8
- **Verified by:** a test with people (the walk-first test script)

### X5 Methods are extensions

Method-specific parts (Lean Six Sigma and control charts, metric expressions, economic appraisal, a particular evaluation framework) are extensions outside the core kinds and the first-use interface, off unless turned on.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** No cross-domain source asks for them in a core; today they sit in it.
- **Domains:** every domain read
- **Sources:** [g5](../../../research/round-3/g5-current-state.md) C22; [g6](../../../research/round-3/g6-domains.md) section 8; [g2](../../../research/round-3/g2-evaluation.md) section 8
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### X6 Hold results, link to tools

Cartograph holds the result of a domain's own analysis (an appraisal, a building model, a backlog) and links to the tool that made it, rather than absorbing it.

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Absorbing each domain's analysis would make the core every domain's tool and no one's.
- **Domains:** construction, software and services, public sector
- **Sources:** [g6](../../../research/round-3/g6-domains.md) section 8
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### X7 The organisation's calendar

Reporting calendars follow the organisation (fiscal year, quarter, season, term, sprint, custom periods).

- **Priority:** Must
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** Reporting cycles differ by domain and country.
- **Domains:** every domain read
- **Sources:** [g6](../../../research/round-3/g6-domains.md) section 4
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records

### X8 More than one language

Text can be held in more than one language.

- **Priority:** Should
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Why:** IATI and Canada carry text in several languages.
- **Domains:** public sector, international development
- **Sources:** [g4](../../../research/round-3/g4-first-principles.md) section 5
- **Verified by:** inspection of the model and its records
