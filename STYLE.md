# Style

## Writing

- Write for the reader's job. Tutorials and how-to guides address the reader as "you"; explanation discusses and weighs; reference states facts and nothing else ([Diataxis](https://diataxis.fr/)).
- Plain words. Define a term once, in [the glossary](docs/reference/glossary.md), and use it the same way everywhere.
- No em dashes: use a colon, a comma or two sentences.
- No sizes or durations as rules: Cartograph serves a hobbyist and a nation alike.
- Cite every claim from a source by its bibliography key, and paraphrase; quote at most one short passage per source.
- A requirement is a statement that can be checked, with the conditions under which it is met.
- No real client's names or documents.

## Diagrams

Mermaid, in the page. In flowcharts, quote every label and use no pipes. Use a diagram where a structure or a flow is clearer seen than read.

## Commit messages
