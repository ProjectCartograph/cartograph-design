# Style

## Writing

- Plain words, second person in tutorials and how-to guides, a conversational tone in explanation, dry facts in reference ([Diataxis](https://diataxis.fr/)).
- No em dashes: use a colon, a comma or two sentences (`just words`).
- No sizes or durations as rules: Cartograph serves a hobbyist and a nation alike.
- Paraphrase sources, with at most one short quotation each, and link them.
- A requirement is a statement that can be checked: "A person can...", "The record holds...", "Each decision...".
- Name the domain a claim comes from; say when a source was read only second-hand.
- No real client's names or documents.

## Diagrams

Mermaid, in the page. In flowcharts, quote every label and use no pipes. Use a diagram where structure or flow is easier seen than read, not as decoration.

Google's Angular format, as in the other Cartograph repositories: see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
