# Shared brief, part three: design criteria for a rebuild, if one is better

Read ../COMMON.md (the product, the users, Diátaxis, sources, copyright,
words) first.

## The question

The owner is open to a **complete rebuild** of Cartograph once there are
excellent, high-quality design criteria. Do not retrofit an ideal onto
what exists: if a better structure is found, say so plainly. The
current product may have the wrong centre, the wrong nouns, or sections
that are irrelevant. Equally, keep what is genuinely good. Nothing is
sacred and nothing is condemned in advance.

The owner points to the UK government's frameworks as a solid base: the
Green Book and its business case guidance, the Magenta Book, and the
others. Use them as primary sources, and check them against
non-UK equivalents so the result does not fit one country only:
Cartograph serves a hobbyist, a cooperative, an NGO, a ministry, a
nation.

## What every agent produces

Your brief names one file. In it:
1. What your sources say, accurately, with section or paragraph numbers
   and URLs, read at source where at all possible (gov.uk assets are
   usually fetchable; extract PDFs to text and read them). Say plainly
   what you could not read.
2. **Design criteria** that follow from them: each one a requirement a
   tool for defining and steering work should meet, phrased so it can be
   checked ("A person can ... ", "The record holds ... ", "Each decision
   ... "), with its source, and marked essential or desirable.
3. Where the frameworks disagree or leave room, the options, with the
   trade-off.
4. Anything in Cartograph today that the sources make look irrelevant,
   misplaced or missing (read the repositories: the engine's contract,
   TAXONOMY.md, the interface's copy and routes, and the research and
   guide from rounds one and two under ../).

## Rules

As in COMMON.md. No sizes or durations as rules. Paraphrase; one short
quotation per source at most. Write only your file under
~/git/project-cartograph/.eval/research/greenfield/. Reply with just its path.
