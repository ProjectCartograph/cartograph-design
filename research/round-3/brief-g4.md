# G4: first principles, people and data standards

Sources: Diátaxis (the map as a model of how people use a product);
the GOV.UK Service Manual on user needs and service design; Nielsen
Norman Group or equivalent on mental models, information architecture
and progressive disclosure; open data standards for describing work and
its results so the record is not locked in a tool: IATI (the
International Aid Transparency Initiative activity standard), the Open
Contracting Data Standard, 360Giving, and any open standard for
indicators or results you can find at source.

Focus: from first principles, what a person who wants to "write down
what we decided to do and why, and steer it" actually needs, across the
kinds of user in COMMON.md and the Diátaxis needs. Propose two or three
genuinely different **centres** a rebuilt Cartograph could have (for
example: the decision and its case; the theory of change; the portfolio
of work; something else), with what each makes easy and hard. Say what
an open data standard would require of the record so it can be shared
and exchanged. Write the file g4-first-principles.md.
