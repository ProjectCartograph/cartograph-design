# G2: the Magenta Book and evaluation by design

Sources: HM Treasury, The Magenta Book (central government guidance on
evaluation) and its annexes and supplementary guides (handling
complexity in policy evaluation, realist evaluation, quality of
evaluation, the evaluation and monitoring frameworks); the Green Book
on monitoring and evaluation; the OECD DAC evaluation criteria
(relevance, coherence, effectiveness, efficiency, impact,
sustainability) and their 2019 definitions; and one well-established
results framework (logframe or theory of change guidance from a
development agency).

Focus: how evaluation and monitoring should be **designed in from the
start** rather than added later: the theory of change and logic model,
evaluation questions, the type of evaluation (process, impact, value
for money), proportionality, the data that must be planned before
delivery starts, baselines, counterfactuals, and how findings feed back
into decisions. Write the file g2-evaluation.md.
