# G3: delivery, risk, assurance and the life cycle

Sources: GovS 002 Project delivery; the Teal Book (project delivery
guidance, projectdelivery.gov.uk); the Orange Book (management of risk,
principles and risk appetite guidance); the Aqua Book (quality
assurance of analysis); the Infrastructure and Projects Authority's
Project Routemap and its gate reviews (gates 0 to 5 and their
questions); the IPA's benefits management guidance; the Government
Service Manual and Service Standard (discovery, alpha, beta, live);
the Rose Book (knowledge assets) only if it bears on the record a tool
keeps.

Focus: how these frameworks **chain together over a piece of work's
whole life**: the stages and gates, who decides at each, what assurance
looks at, how risk is owned and escalated, how benefits are tracked
after delivery, and what records each stage leaves. Map the life cycle
from idea to benefits realised, and say which record a tool must keep
at each point. Write the file g3-life-cycle.md.
