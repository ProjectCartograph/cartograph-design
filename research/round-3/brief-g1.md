# G1: the Green Book and the business case

Sources: HM Treasury, The Green Book (2026 edition) and its
supplementary guidance (optimism bias, discounting, wellbeing, place
based analysis, distributional analysis where relevant); HM Treasury,
Guidance on developing business cases for projects and programmes (the
five case model; strategic outline case, outline business case, full
business case; spending objectives; the options framework and the long
list filter; critical success factors; the preferred way forward);
the Welsh Government and NHS editions where they add something; New
Zealand Treasury's Better Business Cases and one other non-UK
equivalent (Australian, Canadian or World Bank project appraisal).

Focus: the **structure of a decision to invest** over time: what is
decided at each stage, what must be known by then, what is deferred,
who approves; how options are generated, filtered and compared; how
the case stays alive after approval (benefits, review). Write the file
g1-business-case.md.
