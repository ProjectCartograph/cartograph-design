# Research

The evidence behind the design: three rounds of reports and a fourth note, each written by a separate research agent from a shared brief and a brief of its own, kept beside the reports. How the research was done, and how far to trust it, is in [how the research was done](../docs/explanation/how-the-research-was-done.md).

The reports are kept as they were written, as a record. Some refer to files in cartograph-engine and cartograph-ui, or to working folders, as they stood on 2026-10-17; those links are not checked. Each report opens with how far each of its sources was read.
