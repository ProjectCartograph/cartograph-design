# Agent F: portfolios

Research what setting up and running a portfolio involves, from: PMI's
Standard for Portfolio Management (4th edition: strategic alignment,
capacity and capability, value, risk, governance); AXELOS/PeopleCert
Management of Portfolios (MoP: portfolio definition and delivery cycles,
categorisation, prioritisation, balancing, the portfolio strategy and
delivery plan); ISO 21504:2022 (portfolio management guidance); APM on
portfolio management; GovS 002 and HM Treasury on portfolios and
spending decisions; and well-documented prioritisation methods (scoring
against strategic criteria, weighted shortest job first where sourced,
efficient frontier). Cover how a portfolio decides what to start, stop,
pause and continue, how it balances risk and time, and what records it
keeps about each decision.

Write portfolios.md with the same six parts as the programme brief
(what a portfolio definition holds by source with a table; essential,
common, later; how scale and long horizons change it; what differs from
a programme and a project; Cartograph against it, reading the Portfolio,
PortfolioDecisions, Allocation and FundingSource schemas, guidance,
TAXONOMY decisions and the portfolio screens; the Diátaxis needs of a
portfolio manager, an investment board member and a strategy office).

Output: ~/git/project-cartograph/.eval/research/scale/portfolios.md
